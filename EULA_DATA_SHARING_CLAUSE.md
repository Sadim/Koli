# Data Sharing & Pooling — Draft Clause (Standalone Opt-In)

**DRAFT ONLY — NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** This is a starting point for an attorney
to review, revise, and formally incorporate into a full End User License
Agreement / Terms of Service. Do not publish or rely on this text as-is.
Scoping choices below (what's shared vs. excluded) are recommendations,
not decisions — flag anything you want changed before this goes to counsel.

## Purpose

[Company Name] offers optional data pooling that improves creator-brand
matching accuracy across the community of users who choose to
participate. This is a standalone choice, available regardless of
subscription tier — never a condition of payment, never bundled with
any paid plan. Choosing not to share data does not affect access to any
paid features.

## What is shared (opt-in, available to any user regardless of plan)

Only the following categories of data collected through the Service may
be shared into the pooled intelligence layer, and only for customers who
have affirmatively enabled the Paid Tier data-sharing feature:

- Aggregate sponsor-detection data: brand name, detected niche/category,
  mention frequency, and approximate timing — not the specific comment
  or transcript excerpt that produced the detection
- Channel-level classification signals: niche category, approximate
  audience-size tier, posting cadence
- Engagement-authenticity scores (numeric only, not underlying comment text)

## What is explicitly excluded from pooling

The following are never included in the shared pool, regardless of tier:

- Creator contact information — email addresses, phone numbers, or any
  personal contact detail
- The customer's own outreach records — Outreach status, Last Contact,
  Notes, deal terms, or any Draft Deal Memo content
- Raw comment text, video transcripts, or any verbatim third-party content
- Any data the customer has manually entered or corrected, unless the
  customer separately and explicitly opts that specific correction into
  the shared pool

## Consent mechanism

- Data sharing is opt-in, not opt-out, and independent of subscription
  tier — a dedicated toggle in Settings, not something enabled by
  upgrading or purchasing anything.
- [Company Name] will present a clear, plain-language summary of what is
  and is not shared at the point of consent, separate from the general
  Terms of Service — not buried in a single omnibus document.
- Customers may withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal stops future
  contributions but does not retroactively remove data already pooled
  and aggregated as of the withdrawal date. **[Confirm with counsel
  whether retroactive deletion should be offered or required — this is
  jurisdiction-dependent, e.g. GDPR Article 17 right to erasure.]**

## Use of pooled data

Pooled data is used solely to improve matching recommendations and
sponsor-intelligence features shown back to Paid Tier customers. It is
not sold to third parties and is not used to build profiles of
individual creators beyond the aggregate signals described above.

## Note for counsel — third-party data

The data described above pertains to creators and brands who are not
parties to this Agreement and have not separately consented to inclusion
in the pool. The legal basis for processing this third-party data, and
any jurisdiction-specific obligations (e.g., a GDPR legitimate-interest
assessment, CCPA/CPRA disclosures, or equivalent), should be assessed
directly by counsel before this clause is finalized or the feature is
launched. This is precisely the part of the plan that most needs a
professional's sign-off, not a template.
