What Koli is — and isn't

Koli is Apps Script code that runs attached to your own Google Sheet, under your own Google account. The developer of Koli does not operate a backend service that your data passes through: analysis runs using API keys you provide, and results are written directly into your own spreadsheet and Drive. The developer never receives, stores, or has access to your spreadsheet data. See the Privacy Policy for full detail on data handling.

Your own API keys and costs

Koli requires you to supply your own YouTube Data API key and your own AI provider key (Gemini, Mistral, or Groq).
