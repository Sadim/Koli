What Koli does

Koli is a Google Sheets add-on for running an influencer-marketing agency's research and outreach: analyzing YouTube channels/videos, tracking sponsor activity, scoring brand fit, and drafting outreach emails, entirely inside a spreadsheet you own. Koli runs as Apps Script code attached to your own Google Sheet, under your own Google account. There is no separate company server: the developer of Koli does not operate any backend that your data passes through.

Access Koli requests, and why

ScopeWhat it's used for
spreadsheets.currentonlyRead and write the one spreadsheet Koli is attached to — never any other spreadsheet in your Drive.
script.container.uiShow Koli's menu, sidebar, and dialogs inside the Sheets UI.
script.external_requestCall the YouTube Data API and your chosen AI provider (Gemini/Mistral/Groq) using the API key(s) you enter in Settings.
userinfo.emailIdentify which account is running Koli, for the license/trial check described below.
documentsGenerate exported documents (Creator One-Pager, Deal Memo, Shortlist Report, Performance Report) as Google Docs you can edit.
drive.fileSave those generated documents/PDFs into a "Koli Reports" folder Koli creates in your Drive. This scope only ever sees files Koli itself creates — not your existing Drive contents.
script.send_mailSend an outreach email on your behalf, and only when you explicitly click "Send Approved Drafts" after reviewing it. This scope can only send mail; it cannot read your inbox, contacts, or any existing message.

What's stored, and where

Your YouTube/Gemini/Mistral/Groq API keys, and an optional premium access code, are stored in this spreadsheet's Apps Script Properties — a storage area tied to your own copy of the script, never transmitted to the developer or any third party except when used to call the API they belong to. A hashed (not plaintext) form of the access code, plus the date you first entered it, is stored the same way, solely to check trial/premium status locally.

Everything else Koli produces — channel/video analysis, sponsor records, outreach drafts, generated reports — is written directly into your own spreadsheet or your own Drive. None of it passes through, or is retained by, any server the developer operates.

Third-party API calls Koli makes on your behalf

Using your own API key(s), Koli sends the following to external services strictly to produce the analysis you requested:

  • YouTube Data API (Google): channel/video IDs and public metadata (titles, descriptions, comments, statistics) — all already public information.
  • Your chosen AI provider (Google Gemini, Mistral, or Groq): the same public channel/video text, sent for niche/authenticity/audience analysis.
  • SponsorBlock and Reddit (public, unauthenticated endpoints): a video ID or channel name, to check for crowdsourced sponsor-segment data or brand-safety signals. No account or key is used for either.

Koli does not control these third parties' own data practices; see their respective privacy policies for how they handle a request once it reaches them.

What Koli does NOT do

Does not operate a server that receives or stores your data
Does not collect analytics or telemetry on your usage
Does not sell, share, or transmit your data to any third party beyond the API calls above
Does not read, send, or manage email beyond an explicit, per-send "Send Approved Drafts" click
